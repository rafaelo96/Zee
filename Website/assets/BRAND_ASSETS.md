# Third-party brand assets

These files are embedded without visual modification in the Zee product site:

- Lucide icons (inline in `index.html`) — the interface icons on the feature cards and lists, from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 0.460.0 under the ISC License; the license notice is kept beside the symbols and credited in the site footer.
- `badges/studio.js` — the nine templates' badges and the heraldic figures as data, written from the app's source so the page can draw badges in each style.
- `badges/*.svg` — Badge Studio's nine Space templates, drawn from the app's badge geometry and palettes. The figures are game-icons.net artwork by Lorc under CC BY 3.0 (credited in the site footer and `ZeeShared/Resources/HeraldicArtworkCredits.txt`). The Sun and River figures and Meadow's laurel trim are SF Symbols in the app and are redrawn here as plain shapes.

The surrounding labels and destinations identify the third-party service without implying sponsorship or affiliation.
