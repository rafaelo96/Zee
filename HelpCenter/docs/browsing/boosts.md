# Restyle sites with Boosts

A Boost changes how one site looks for you. It applies to the site and every subdomain of it, so a Boost for `youtube.com` also reaches `m.youtube.com`, but never a site whose name only looks alike. Boosts work in both Chromium and WebKit on Mac, and sync to your other devices with the rest of the Space.

## Make a Boost

Open **Site Controls** on the site and choose **Boost This Site**. Then, in the editor below it:

- **Background**, **Text** and **Links**: pick a color, or the crossed-out circle to keep the site's own.
- **Font** and **Text Size**: give the site's text another font and size.
- **Hide an Element…**: point at what you want gone and choose **Hide**. Hidden elements are listed under it; remove one to show it again. **Page › Hide Element with Boost** does the same from the menu bar or the command palette.
- **Use in Every Space**: apply the Boost in all your Spaces instead of only the one you made it in.
- **Advanced**: add your own CSS and JavaScript, then choose **Apply**. Your CSS comes last, so it wins over the rest. Your script runs each time a page of the site opens, in a world of its own that the site can't see; reload the page to run a changed script.

Turn a Boost off with its switch, or delete it with **Delete Boost**.

## Manage your Boosts

Each Space lists its Boosts in **Settings**, on the Space's **Browsing** page, where you can turn them on or off and delete them. A Boost goes with its Space when you export your browser data, and is removed when you delete the Space.
